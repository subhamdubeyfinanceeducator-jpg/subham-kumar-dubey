About

Subham Kumar Dubey brings an academic background across commerce, finance and law, with a steady interest in connecting legal knowledge to financial understanding and practical decision-making.


He holds a B.Com from DDU Gorakhpur University, an M.Com from RMLAU Ayodhya, and an LL.B. from MPU Balrampur, Uttar Pradesh. His professional interests centre on finance, legal research, workplace issues, business matters, financial education and public awareness.


He is also building his voice as a content creator, speaker and motivator through research-based educational content. His main projects, Courtroom Files and Business Files, aim to simplify complex legal, employment, business and financial topics for ordinary people and professionals alike.
